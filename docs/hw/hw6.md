## Objectives

This assignment asks you to explore definitions and examples of open-source software and proprietary software, together with their licenses.

Minimal knowledge and experience to acquire include the following:
* Definition of FOSS, contrast with proprietary software.
* Definition of copyleft, contrast with copyright.
* Know examples of copyleft and permissive licenses, including MIT, GPL3, Apache, and CC-by-SA.
* Know examples of software used in this class and their licenses: Linux kernel, Apache web server, Docker Engine, Microsoft Teams, Git, Visual Studio Code, VSCodium.

Wikipedia has excellent information on all of the above.

## Likely quiz questions

* What is the difference between FOSS and proprietary software?
* Define _copyleft_.
* Give two examples of a copyleft license.
* Give two examples of a permissive license.
* Give three examples of software used in this class and their licenses. The examples must include one permissive license, one copyleft license, and one proprietary license.
