# Preparation for and participation in discussions

* Discussion sessions in this course are 100% device-free.
* You should bring with you to the discussion handwritten notes made with pen/pencil and paper. You can refer to these during the discussion. The reading itself, whether in paper or electronic form, will not be available during the discussion.
* The instructor will briefly glance over your handwritten notes before the session begins to get a rough assessment of whether the notes are substantive. The level of detail is up to you, but a suggested guideline is to aim for half a page of notes for a 30-minute discussion. (Thirty minutes is the typical length of our discussions.)

## Achieving a satisfactory level of participation and preparation

To achieve a satisfactory level of participation and preparation, the following two requirements should be met:

* Substantial handwritten notes are prepared.
* One or more contributions made during the discussion demonstrate a genuine attempt to understand the assigned reading.  
  * Contributions should be of an appropriate length, and the instructor will provide real-time guidance on this.
  * Long comments are not valuable to the discussion because they prevent others from contributing.
  * Very short comments are not useful because they do not demonstrate your understanding of and insight into the reading.

## Make-up Discussion Sessions

It is possible to make up for a missed or unsatisfactorydiscussion session, by meeting with the instructor during _scheduled office hours_. Be sure to complete this makeup session within 10 days of the missed discussion; otherwise, an unsatisfactory progress warning will likely be issued.
